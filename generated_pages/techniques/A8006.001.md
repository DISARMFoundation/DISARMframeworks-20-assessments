# Assessment A8006.001: Direct Recipient of Activity

**Summary**: Summary: The activity was delivered to, or carried out on, the participant individually.<br><br>Detail: A8006.001: Direct Recipient of Activity records that activity was directly carried out on a subject. For example, they received a message, were tagged in a post, or physically attacked. The distinction for this assessment is that the subject was directly interacted with by the actor.<br><br>Example<br><br>On 20 and 21 October 2020, in the run-up to the United States presidential election, [emails were sent to registered voters in Florida, Alaska, and Arizona.](https://www.osi.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/2395806/bulletin-iranian-spoofed-emails-portraying-the-proud-boys/) [Each was addressed to a named individual, cited that person's own registration details, and claimed the sender was "in possession of all your information."](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2020/10/20/proud-boys-emails-florida/) The messages told the recipient to vote for Donald Trump and threatened consequences if they did not, and were presented as coming from the Proud Boys. [The Office of the Director of National Intelligence and the FBI attributed the operation to Iran on 21 October 2020.](https://www.politifact.com/article/2020/oct/22/federal-officials-say-iran-sent-email-threats-vote/)<br><br>This case demonstrates direct delivery in a state-linked operation. Each recipient was addressed individually from their own registration record, so every voter who received an email is a direct recipient. People who learned of the campaign from the news coverage which followed take A8006.002: Observer of Activity instead, which separates the two values inside one operation.

**Category**: TA92 Actor Analysis <br><br>**Parent Assessment:** A8006 Participant Exposure
