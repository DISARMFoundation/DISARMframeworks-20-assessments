# Assessment A1012.003: Subject's Physical Health Deteriorates

**Summary**: Summary: Activity has the assessed impact of causing a decline in the subject's physical health without direct bodily injury.<br><br>Detail: This impact records physical health consequences which arise from the experience of being targeted rather than from any immediate physical injury.<br><br>A subject's physical health can deteriorate as a consequence of consuming misleading health information. Further, sustained harassment, surveillance, and threats produce documented physical effects, including sleep disruption, cardiovascular symptoms, appetite and weight change, and worsening of existing conditions.<br><br>Example<br><br>From mid-2021, claims circulated widely online that the antiparasitic drug ivermectin prevented or treated COVID-19, a use for which it is not authorised. [Outpatient dispensing reached more than 88,000 prescriptions in the week ending 13 August 2021,](https://archive.cdc.gov/emergency_cdc_gov/han/2021/han00449.asp) twenty-four times the pre-pandemic baseline, and [some people obtained veterinary formulations intended for large animals,](https://archive.cdc.gov/emergency_cdc_gov/han/2021/han00449.asp) which can be highly concentrated and cause overdoses when used by humans. [Calls to United States poison control centres for human ivermectin exposure had tripled against baseline by January 2021 and were running at five times baseline by July;](https://www.cnn.com/2021/08/26/health/ivermectin-covid-19-warning-injuries) emergency department and hospital visits rose correspondingly, and the CDC issued a health advisory on 26 August 2021 describing the resulting illness as severe. The Oregon Poison Center alone took [21 calls in August 2021 from people who had taken ivermectin for COVID-19.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34670041/) Six were hospitalised, four of them in intensive care, with reported effects including gastrointestinal distress, hypotension, confusion, ataxia, and seizures.
