# Assessment A1012.008: Subject Physically Detained

**Summary**: Summary: The subject has been deprived of liberty and held in custody in connection with or as a result of activity.<br><br>Detail: This impact records that the subject was taken into custody. It covers detention by state authorities with or without legal process, imprisonment following conviction, and detention by non-state actors.<br><br>Custody is the defining feature. A subject who is prevented from leaving a country, whose passport is confiscated, or who is placed under a bail condition has had their freedom restricted without being taken into custody, and that is a different outcome recorded elsewhere.<br><br>The detained person is often not the person an operation is aimed at. Detention of relatives is a standard coercion by proxy mechanism, and the detention of a journalist's family in their country of origin is an impact of a campaign directed at the journalist abroad. Recording it requires attributing an outcome suffered by one person to activity targeting another.<br><br>Detention rarely concludes a sequence. Loss of employment, financial loss, deterioration in health, and damage to the subject's relationships follow from it routinely, and each is a separate outcome in its own right.<br><br>Example<br><br>Gulchehra Hoja is a Uyghur journalist with Radio Free Asia's Uyghur Service who reports on detention in Xinjiang. [On 28 January 2018 she published an interview with a survivor of the internment camps, and three days later, Chinese authorities detained twenty-four more of her relatives.](https://focustaiwan.tw/culture/202607210008) [Her brother had previously been arrested in 2017 and spent three years in an internment camp; her mother was detained in 2018 and held for two months.](https://hrf.org/latest/chinese-communist-party-stop-coercing-family-of-uyghur-journalist-gulchehra-hoja/) Her parents have repeatedly been refused passports in retaliation for her work, and [Chinese authorities published a video in which her mother and brother were coerced into stating on camera that they were living normal lives.](https://hrf.org/latest/chinese-communist-party-stop-coercing-family-of-uyghur-journalist-gulchehra-hoja/) Rights groups, including Amnesty International, separately documented around [twenty relatives detained in the same period, some over contact with Hoja in a WeChat group, and condemned the pattern as retaliation against her reporting.](https://www.rfa.org/english/news/uyghur/reporters-relatives-03012018164751.html)
