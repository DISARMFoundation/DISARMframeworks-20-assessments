# Assessment A8004.003: Unintended Subject of Activity

**Summary**: Summary: The subject is assessed to have been impacted without the actor having intended to impact them.<br><br>Detail: A8004.003: Unintended Subject of Activity records a participant was impacted by activity, but was not intentionally selected for impact by the actor on deciding to conduct an incident.<br><br>A subject targeted as part of a category or demographic the actor was aiming at is not an unintended subject. An actor who set out to impact a community intended to impact its members, so a member of that community takes A8004.001: Intended Target of Activity even though nobody identified them by name.<br><br>A participant attacked in the belief that they were somebody else takes this value together with A8006.001: Direct Recipient of Activity.<br><br>Example<br><br>On 28 December 2017 Tyler Barriss telephoned police in Wichita, Kansas, and falsely reported a shooting and kidnapping at an address in the city. [An Ohio gamer had recruited him to target a Wichita gamer after a dispute over a $1.50 bet in Call of Duty: WWII, but the address they used was out of date and led police to Andrew Finch, who was not involved in the game or the dispute. Finch answered the door and an officer shot him dead. Barriss was sentenced to 20 years in prison in March 2019.](https://www.ktvu.com/news/california-man-sentenced-to-20-years-in-fatal-swatting-call-over-dispute-about-call-of-duty)<br><br>This case demonstrates impact arriving through a mistaken identification. The actors selected another player, Shane Gaskill, and the police response reached Finch because the address was wrong rather than because anyone selected him. Finch takes A8004.003: Unintended Subject of Activity together with A8006.001: Direct Recipient of Activity.

**Category**: TA92 Actor Analysis <br><br>**Parent Assessment:** A8004 Targeting Intentionality
